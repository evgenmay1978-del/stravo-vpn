## Промпт

Create a production illustration asset for the STRAVO VPN Android UI, wide 3:1 aspect ratio, 2048x680 if possible. A very refined graphite pencil engraving on TRANSPARENT background: layered rugged alpine ridges and valleys along the very bottom edge, slightly taller peaks framing the bottom left and bottom right, a low open valley at bottom center, extremely fine hand-drawn cross-hatching like an antique architectural expedition notebook. Delicate orbital drafting arcs and two tiny hand-shaded spheres at the upper corners. Sophisticated expensive stationery / artisan instrument aesthetic. Monochrome graphite ink only, no green, no gold. Top 60 percent is entirely empty transparent negative space, artwork concentrates in bottom 30 percent and feathered edges dissolve upward, so real native interface text and cards can remain readable above it. No paper fill, no solid white rectangle, use actual alpha channel transparency. No lettering, no numbers, no logos, no UI, no buttons, no phone device, no border. This is a subtle background layer, not a wallpaper photo: meticulous light pencil lines, restrained shadows, original artwork.
